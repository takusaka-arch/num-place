Privacy Policy

Sudoku Solving Assistant / Effective: October 28, 2026

Sudoku Solving Assistant (“the App”) handles user information as follows.

1. Information the App collects

The App does not send or collect users’ personal information on the developer’s servers. It does not show ads or run usage analytics.

Puzzles, in-progress states, and settings are stored in the browser on the user’s device (local storage). Reading puzzles from photos is also processed on the device, and images are not sent outside it.

2. Google Drive integration

Only when the user chooses to save to Google Drive does the App ask to connect with a Google account. The App uses the following permission:

  • drive.file: permission to read and write only files the App created or files the user opened with the App. The App cannot see any other files in Google Drive.

With this permission, the App saves and loads puzzle and in-progress files in the “Sudoku” folder of the user’s Google Drive. Data is exchanged directly between the user’s device and Google, without passing through the developer’s servers. The developer never sees users’ files.

To connect automatically next time, the App stores the email address of the most recently connected Google account only in the browser on the user’s device.

The App’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Sharing with third parties

The App does not sell or provide user information to third parties.

4. Disconnecting and deleting data

  • Pressing “Disconnect” in the App removes the account information stored on the device and ends the Google Drive connection.
  • You can revoke the App’s access at any time from “Third-party apps & services” in your Google Account.
  • Files saved to Google Drive can be deleted freely by the user in Google Drive.
  • Data on the device is removed when you clear the site data in your browser.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact takuma.sakai@gmail.com.

6. Changes

If this policy is changed, the change will be announced on this page.
